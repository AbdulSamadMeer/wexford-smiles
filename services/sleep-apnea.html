Sleep apnea & snoring.

Snoring can sometimes be associated with obstructive sleep apnea. If you have concerns about your sleep or breathing, speak with your physician about evaluation and diagnosis.

Oral appliance therapy

Care that works
with your health team.

For some patients, a dentist may provide a custom oral appliance as part of a plan coordinated with their physician. Suitability depends on your diagnosis and individual needs.

Understand your symptoms

Snoring and daytime tiredness can have different causes. A physician can determine whether a sleep evaluation is appropriate and guide diagnosis.

Custom oral appliances

A dental appliance worn during sleep may be considered for some people with snoring or obstructive sleep apnea. Your dentist can explain the fitting process and discuss whether it may be suitable.

Work with your physician

Diagnosis and treatment planning for sleep apnea involve a physician. Dental appliance care can be coordinated with your medical team when appropriate.

Follow-up care

Follow-up helps assess fit, comfort, and the condition of an appliance. Your physician guides medical evaluation of how well sleep apnea is being managed.

Other treatment approaches

There are different ways to manage sleep apnea, including CPAP and other medical treatments. Your physician can help determine which approaches fit your health needs.

Start with a conversation

If you have a sleep apnea diagnosis or a physician’s recommendation, we can discuss whether dental appliance care may be an option for you.
