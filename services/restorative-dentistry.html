Repair & replace

Care to help restore
your everyday smile.

Treatment depends on the tooth, your oral health, and what matters most to you. We’ll take time to talk through the details.

Tooth-colored fillings

When decay creates a cavity, a filling repairs the area after the damaged portion is removed. Composite fillings can be matched to the natural color of your tooth.

Root canal therapy

If the soft tissue inside a tooth is infected or inflamed, root canal treatment may help preserve the tooth. Your dentist will explain what to expect and whether a crown may be recommended afterward.

Dental crowns

A custom crown covers a weakened or damaged tooth to help restore its shape and function. Materials and design depend on the tooth and your needs.

Dental bridges

A bridge can replace a missing tooth by attaching a replacement to neighboring teeth. We’ll review whether this or another option is suitable for your smile.

Implants & dentures

For missing teeth, implants or full and partial dentures may be considered. We can discuss the differences and coordinate care to find an approach that fits.

We’ll help you compare options based on your oral health, comfort, and goals—without rushing the decision.
