Implant treatment is planned in stages and may involve other dental providers. A consultation is the first step to see what may work for you.

Single-tooth implants

An implant replaces a tooth root and can support a custom replacement tooth. Your dentist can explain the steps involved and whether an implant is appropriate for your situation.

Multiple missing teeth

When several teeth are missing, your care team can discuss options such as individual implants, bridges, or dentures based on your needs.

Implant-supported dentures

Implants can help stabilize some dentures. The number and placement of implants depend on your oral health and the type of restoration being considered.

What to expect

Planning may include an exam, imaging, and coordination with a specialist. Healing and treatment timelines vary, so we’ll outline the steps relevant to your care.

Alternatives

Bridges and conventional partial or full dentures may be alternatives. We’ll compare the benefits and considerations of each so you can make an informed choice.

Find the option that fits your goals

We’ll help you understand what’s involved and connect your treatment plan to your priorities and oral health.
