Preventive care

Small steps for
lasting health.

Preventive visits are a chance to clean hard-to-reach areas, look for early changes, and get practical guidance for your daily routine.

Professional cleanings

A hygienist removes plaque and tartar that brushing and flossing can miss, then polishes the tooth surfaces. Your cleaning can be tailored to your needs and comfort.

Dental exams

Routine exams help your dentist check your teeth and gums and spot changes that may need attention. When appropriate, X-rays can help assess areas that aren't visible during an exam.

Gum health

Healthy gums support your teeth. If we notice signs of inflammation or gum disease, we'll explain what we see and talk through next steps, which may include a deeper cleaning.

Home-care guidance

Brushing twice a day and cleaning between your teeth daily help remove plaque. We can share practical tips and recommend products based on your oral health.

Personalized visit schedule

Many people benefit from regular checkups, but the right interval depends on your needs. Your dental team can suggest a schedule that suits your health and history.

Ready to make your next visit?

Whether it's been six months or a little longer, we'll meet you where you are and help you take the next step.
