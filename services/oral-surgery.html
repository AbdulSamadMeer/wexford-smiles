Treatment options

Clear guidance
at every step.

Every situation is different. Your dentist will examine the area and discuss the safest next steps for your oral health.

Wisdom teeth

Wisdom teeth can become impacted or contribute to discomfort and other concerns. An exam can help determine whether monitoring, treatment, or a referral is appropriate.

Tooth infection

If a tooth is severely infected or damaged, your dentist will assess whether it can be saved with restorative treatment or if extraction should be considered.

Routine extractions

When removing a tooth is recommended, we’ll explain what to expect, discuss comfort options, and provide instructions for caring for the area afterward.

Aftercare & recovery

Recovery instructions depend on your procedure and health. We’ll give you guidance on keeping the area clean, what to expect, and when to contact us with concerns.
