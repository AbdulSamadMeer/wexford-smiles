For parents

Healthy habits
start early.

Regular visits help children get comfortable with dental care and give families a chance to ask questions as little smiles grow.

When should my child first visit?

A first dental visit is often recommended around the first birthday or when the first tooth appears. Your dentist can suggest a schedule that suits your child.

How often should children have checkups?

Many children benefit from regular visits about every six months. Your child’s dentist may recommend a different schedule based on their individual needs.

How do I care for baby teeth?

Clean your baby’s gums with a soft cloth or infant toothbrush. As teeth appear, brush gently with a soft-bristled brush and ask your dentist about age-appropriate toothpaste.

Are baby teeth important?

Yes. Baby teeth help with chewing and speech and hold space for adult teeth. Keeping them healthy supports your child as they grow.

How can I protect teeth during sports?

A mouthguard can help protect teeth and soft tissues during sports. Ask us whether a custom-fitted option may be right for your child.

What if my child has a toothache?

Call our team for guidance and an appointment. If your child has significant swelling, severe pain, or an injury, seek prompt dental care.

Tell us what helps your child feel comfortable, and we’ll do our best to make the visit calm and welcoming.
