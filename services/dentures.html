Comfortable replacement options

Dentures for everyday confidence.

Full and partial dentures can replace missing teeth and help restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile. We’ll help you compare options and work toward a comfortable fit.

Talk about dentures ↗

Full & partial options

A fit shaped
around you.

The right denture depends on how many teeth are missing, your oral health, and your preferences. An exam helps us recommend options to consider.

Full dentures

A full denture replaces all teeth in an arch. After teeth are removed, the timing of a conventional denture depends on healing and your treatment plan.

Immediate dentures

An immediate denture may be placed when teeth are removed. As your gums and jaw heal and change shape, adjustments or a reline may be needed.

Partial dentures

A partial denture replaces some missing teeth while fitting around the remaining teeth. Designs and materials vary, and we can discuss the differences with you.

Flexible partials

Flexible partial dentures are a removable option made from a flexible material. Your dentist can help determine whether this type of denture is suitable for your mouth.

Fit & adjustments

Dentures can take time to get used to and may need adjustments. Let us know if a denture feels uncomfortable or has become loose so we can assess the fit.

Implant-supported options

For some people, implants can help support a denture. We’ll explain how these options compare and whether a referral or additional evaluation is needed.

Let’s find your comfortable fit

We’ll walk you through full, partial, and implant-supported options and answer your questions along the way.
