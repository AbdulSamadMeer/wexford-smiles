Cosmetic dentistry.

Want to change something about your smile? We’ll listen to what you have in mind and walk you through options that suit your teeth, your goals, and your timeline.

Talk through your options ↗

Smile enhancements

Thoughtful options.
Natural-looking care.

A consultation helps us understand what you’d like to change and whether cosmetic treatment is a good fit for your oral health.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening can help lift some surface and age-related stains. Your dentist can discuss in-office or take-home approaches and whether whitening is appropriate for your teeth.

Bonding

Tooth-colored composite resin can be shaped to improve the appearance of some chips, gaps, or uneven edges. Your dentist can explain the process and how it compares with other options.

Porcelain veneers

Thin porcelain coverings may change the appearance of selected teeth. A dental exam helps determine whether veneers are suitable or if another treatment would better protect your smile.

Inlays, onlays & crowns

When a tooth needs extra support, a custom restoration may improve its appearance while restoring strength. The right option depends on how much of the tooth needs repair.

Bridges

A fixed bridge may replace one or more missing teeth using nearby teeth for support. We can compare a bridge with other tooth replacement options during a consultation.

Let’s find an option that feels right

There’s no one-size-fits-all smile. We’ll explain the benefits, considerations, and alternatives before you decide.
