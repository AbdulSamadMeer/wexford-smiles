Restorative dentistry

Durable solutions for
a strong smile.

Crowns and bridges are custom-made restorations that protect your teeth and replace missing ones, restoring both function and appearance.

Dental crowns

A crown caps a damaged or weakened tooth, protecting it and restoring its shape and strength. Crowns are often used after root canal treatment or to cover large fillings.

Dental bridges

A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to adjacent teeth. It restores your bite and prevents remaining teeth from shifting.

Custom design

Your crown or bridge is carefully designed and colored to match your natural teeth, creating a seamless restoration that feels and looks like your own teeth.

The process

Restoration typically takes two visits. During the first, we prepare the tooth and take impressions. At the second visit, we place your custom restoration.

Durability and care

With good oral hygiene and regular checkups, crowns and bridges can last many years. We'll discuss care tips to protect your investment.
