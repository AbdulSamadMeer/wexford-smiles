save your tooth.

Root canal treatment removes infected or inflamed pulp from inside the tooth, eliminating pain and saving your natural tooth.

When it's needed

Root canal treatment may be recommended when the inner pulp of a tooth becomes infected or inflamed due to decay, trauma, or repeated procedures.

Pain relief

Despite its reputation, root canal treatment actually relieves pain by removing the infected tissue causing discomfort. We focus on making the process as comfortable as possible.

The procedure

The dentist removes the infected pulp, cleans and shapes the canal, then fills and seals it. Local anesthesia ensures comfort throughout the treatment.

Aftercare

Following treatment, we'll provide clear instructions for care. Most teeth need a crown afterward to restore full function and strength.

Success rates

Root canal treatment has a high success rate and allows you to keep your natural tooth. This is often preferable to extraction.
