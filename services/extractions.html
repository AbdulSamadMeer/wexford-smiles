Tooth removal

Comfortable care when
extraction is needed.

Tooth extractions are sometimes necessary to protect your overall oral health. We'll explain the reason and guide you through the process with care and comfort.

When extractions are needed

Extractions may be recommended for severely decayed teeth, advanced gum disease, impacted teeth, or teeth that are preventing other treatment.

Simple extractions

Simple extractions are for teeth visible in the mouth. Local anesthesia ensures the procedure is comfortable and you won't feel pain, though you may feel gentle pressure.

Surgical extractions

More complex cases may require surgical extraction. We'll discuss the specifics and explain what to expect during your consultation.

Aftercare and healing

We'll provide detailed aftercare instructions to promote proper healing and minimize discomfort. This includes guidance on rest, medication, and diet in the following days.

Tooth replacement options

After extraction, we can discuss options for replacing the missing tooth, such as implants, bridges, or dentures, to restore your smile.

Comfortable, caring extraction care

Our team is here to ensure your comfort and successful healing after extraction.
